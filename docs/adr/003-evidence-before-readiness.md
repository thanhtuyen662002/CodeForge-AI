# ADR-003 — evidence and uncertainty before readiness labels

Status: accepted baseline, 2026-10-02.

Decision: store append-only, versioned evidence; separate assistance from independent assessment; de-duplicate families within a defined window; unknown stays null. Initial smoothed estimate is an explainable heuristic, not psychometric validation. Mastery requires diverse independent observations across sessions and delayed recall plus prerequisites.

Do not infer admission probability, employment ability or official certification. Radar/summary must show coverage and count. Job Ready labels wait for broader reviewed performance rubric and calibration.

Tradeoff: fewer flattering instant percentages and slower initial confidence. Benefit: lower risk of misleading a learner or rewarding hint farming. Revisit thresholds using holdout data and subgroup/transfer analysis; retain algorithm version and historical snapshots.
