# AI Practical Talent Assessment Preparation

## Evidence boundary

FACT: VinUni's public AI thực chiến page describes entrance assessment in logic, basic programming/data and practical situations. Its FAQ says the program focuses on practical AI skills and learners may need to review basics themselves. Source checked 2026-10-02: https://vinuni.edu.vn/aithucchien/ . This is the factual motivation for a foundation/preparation track; it does not establish exact questions, timing, weights or pass thresholds.

INFERENCE: Python, SQL, debugging, data handling and AI application fundamentals are useful preparatory capabilities for this product's goal. Their depth/order is our curriculum choice, not a claim about the unpublished selection exam.

PRACTICE CONTENT: All questions, blueprints, mock exams, cases, seeds and scoring thresholds created here are original internal practice content. No endorsement, official affiliation, exclusive exam copying or admission guarantee.

## Stages

0 Diagnostic; 1 Logic & Quantitative Thinking; 2 Programming Fundamentals; 3 Python; 4 SQL; 5 Data Handling; 6 Algorithms & Debugging; 7 AI Fundamentals; 8 LLM & AI Agent; 9 Business AI Cases; 10 Mock Assessment; 11 Final Readiness Test.

Each stage references a versioned set of skills/lessons/assessment blueprints. Skill gap lets learners enter at the right foundation level rather than repeating everything. Progression requires evidence of independent performance, not just watching/reading.

## Example case families — synthetic

Customer support: classify ticket problem, identify data, choose retrieval vs agent, measure answer quality, privacy and fallback. Invoice automation: validation rules, confidence, reconciliation and human review rather than pretending OCR is always correct. Agent workflow: least-privilege tools, retries, idempotency, audit and human approval for consequential actions. Counts and businesses in cases are simulated.
